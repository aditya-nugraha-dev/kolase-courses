# **6. Business Model Definition** 

The MVP is an online Academic English academy delivering structured, small-group instruction with a Cambridge-aligned/CEFR-referenced pedagogical direction, without claiming official Cambridge affiliation, accreditation, endorsement, or certification unless independently authorized in writing. Commercially, the academy uses a free seven-session trial followed by paid 15-, 30-, or 45-session packages. Academically, package length is not equivalent to level or curriculum completion.

# **8. Value Proposition Architecture** 

- Academic clarity: Placement establishes baseline; profiles document strengths, gaps, and next actions. 

- Small-group focus: Standard small group is 3–5 students, target 4; 2-student class is an underfilled exception. 

- Evidence-based progression: Learning objectives, teacher observations, checkpoints, Progress Test and Final Test support decisions. 

- Commercial transparency: Package entitlement, trial credit, price, validity, cancellation and renewal rules are explicit. 

- Operational reliability: Defined SLA, schedule source of truth, audit trails, and service recovery rules.

# **23. Core Business Rules** 

- No package activation before payment verification. 

- No manual Remaining Sessions editing; balances derive from ledger logic. 

- No standard small-group class above 5 students. 

- Standard small-group minimum = 3; 2 students is UNDERFILLED exception; 1 is one-on-one. 

- No official schedule without CONFIRMED status in the scheduling system. 

- No deletion/overwrite of material history as normal process. 

- No academic score/level change by admin without academic authority. 

- No commercial discount/refund/free-session promise outside approved authority. 

- No unverified Cambridge affiliation/certification claims. 

- No silent changes to locked business rules or critical configuration.
